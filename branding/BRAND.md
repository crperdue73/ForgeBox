# ForgeBox — Brand Guide

_Drafted by Del, 2026-10-11. Assets produced by Robbie Perdue._

## Concept

**ForgeBox — a datacenter you strike out of spare iron.**

The name is the whole idea. An anvil, heat, and one hammer-blow that produces a
running machine. This is *metal*, not corporate-blue SaaS. The mood is molten,
dark-first, and terminal-adjacent — it belongs in a rack room, not a boardroom.

## Palette

Dark-first. Any light usage is the inverse exception, not the default.

| Role | Name | Hex | Use |
|---|---|---|---|
| Base | Forge Black | `#1A1714` | Backgrounds, primary surface |
| Primary | Molten Orange | `#FF6B1A` | Brand accent, CTAs, the "strike" |
| Accent | Ember Red | `#C42B1C` | Danger, heat, hover states |
| Secondary | Steel Grey | `#8A9299` | Body text on dark, dividers |
| Highlight | Forge White | `#F5F1EA` | Headlines, key highlights |

Optional extension: a cool **Quench Blue** `#2E7D9A` for "cold" states (success,
inactive) to contrast the heat.

## Logo

- **Mark:** a stylized **anvil** whose body is the silhouette of a **server rack** —
  3–4 horizontal cut lines reading as stacked chassis. At large sizes it reads
  *anvil*; at icon sizes it reads *stack*. That duality is the logo's job.
- **Style:** monoline, cold-stamped, no gradients, no drop shadows.
- **Wordmark:** `FORGEBOX` in a heavy geometric sans, tight tracking, all caps.
- **Variants needed:** full lockup (mark + wordmark), mark-only icon (square + circle
  crop), monochrome (white on black, black on white), and a one-color favicon.
- **Clear space:** one anvil-width of empty space on all sides.

## Typography

- **Display / wordmark:** a heavy geometric sans (e.g. Space Grotesk Bold, Archivo Black).
- **Body:** a clean humanist sans (Inter, Source Sans).
- **Code / commands:** a warm monospace (JetBrains Mono, IBM Plex Mono) — ForgeBox is
  command-line native, so mono is a first-class face.

## Motifs

- **The strike-spark** — the moment of the hammer-blow; sparks flying off the anvil.
- **The rack line** — the repeated horizontal chassis line from the logo.
- **"One command"** — the hero: `docker compose up -d` rendered as the product's promise.

## Wallpapers

1. **Hero (16:9):** the anvil glowing at the instant of the strike, sparks coalescing
   into a single line of command text. Dark, cinematic.
2. **Phone crop (9:16):** the anvil mark centered on Forge Black, faint ember glow.
3. **Minimal:** wordmark `FORGEBOX` bottom-left on Forge Black, anvil mark top-right.

## Voice

Direct, confident, a little hot. "Unleashed." "Strike." "Forge." Short sentences.
Show the outcome, not the internals: *"That unused PC? It's a datacenter."*
